# NovaTech Dynamics - Internal Operations Handbook (2026 Edition)

## 1. Remote Work & Travel Policy
All NovaTech employees are eligible for the "Work-From-Anywhere-Quarter" program. Under this policy, employees may work from any approved international location for up to 90 days per calendar year. NovaTech provides a monthly internet and co-working stipend of $350 USD. Travel reimbursement requires pre-approval from department heads at least 14 business days prior to departure.

## 2. Equipment & Hardware Allowances
Every new team member receives a standard hardware bundle:
- 16-inch custom-configured workstation laptop
- Dual 4K 27-inch monitors
- Ergonomic mechanical keyboard and vertical mouse
- An annual hardware refresh budget of $1,200 USD starting from the second year of tenure.
All hardware is managed via the NovaShield Zero-Trust MDM platform.

## 3. Project Chimera: Project Scope & Key Milestones
Project Chimera is NovaTech's flagship next-generation quantum-resistant encryption mesh. 
Key details:
- Project Lead: Dr. Elena Rostova
- Architecture: Lattice-based cryptography combined with post-quantum key encapsulation mechanism (KEM) named "Aegis-7".
- Deployment Target: Phase 1 alpha rollout is scheduled for November 15, 2026.
- Target latency: Sub-5ms handshake overhead across distributed nodes.

## 4. Wellness, Leave, and Pet Policy
NovaTech observes a 4-day workweek during the summer months (June through August) known as "Summer Fridays Off".
- Annual Leave: 28 days of paid time off (PTO), plus 10 global wellness days.
- In-Office Pet Policy: Dogs and cats are welcome at the headquarters office on Tuesdays and Thursdays ("Paws & Code Days"). Pets must be registered with building facilities and wear an active RFID badge.
- Health Stipend: $150 USD per month for gym memberships, therapy, or fitness equipment.

## 5. Security & Incident Response Protocols
In the event of a suspected credential leak or security anomaly:
1. Immediately execute the internal command `novactl lockdown --scope=local` on your terminal.
2. Notify the Security Operations Center (SOC) on Slack channel `#incident-response-red`.
3. Do not power off your machine; keep memory state intact for forensic analysis.
4. The designated Incident Commander on duty for 2026 is Marcus Vance.
